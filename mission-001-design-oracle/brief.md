# Brief: Mission 001, The Fortune Machine

**Date:** 2026-11-23
*(Written after shipping, from the README.)*

## The idea in one sentence

An 8-bit coin-operated railway fortune machine: drop a coin, watch it work and get a printed ticket that weighs what's on your mind.

## Why this mission

Build a small, working app with a strong 8-bit style using the 8bitcn UI library.

## Who it's for

Anyone, not just designers (V1 was designer-only). Seen mostly through LinkedIn and X posts, often on a phone.

## The core experience

1. Pick what's weighing on you (topic) and what kind of fortune you want (tone).
2. Insert a coin. The coin flies into the slot, the machine wakes up, the bulbs chase and the wheel spins.
3. Tikku, the fortune-keeper, pulls a lever while the printer clicks.
4. A ticket prints out and lands in front of you. Tikku reacts to the fortune.
5. Copy it, save it as a PNG or go again.

## Must have

- The full coin → wheel → printer → ticket ritual in about two seconds.
- 81 original fortunes (3 topics × 3 tones), no repeats until a pool runs out.
- Sound effects, with a mute toggle.
- Copy fortune and save as a 1080×1080 PNG.
- Keyboard and screen-reader support, and a reduced-motion version.
- Responsive from 390px phones to large desktops.

## Out of scope

- Accounts, saving history, backend.

## Done means

The ritual works and feels good on phone and desktop, and the ticket is worth sharing.
